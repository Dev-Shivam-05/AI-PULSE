Transcribe 10 hours of audio for free with faster-whisper

2026-08-30 · SYSTRAN/faster-whisper

The exact command

pip install faster-whisper

What it is

A CTranslate2 reimplementation of OpenAI's Whisper that runs about four times faster on the same CPU.

What to make with it

  • Subtitle a back catalogue of videos overnight
  • Search a year of meeting recordings by keyword
  • Feed a podcast into an LLM as text instead of audio

Skip it if

You need real-time streaming transcription; this is batch-first.
