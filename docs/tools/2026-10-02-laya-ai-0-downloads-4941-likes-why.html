What it is

Laya is a multilingual, non-autoregressive 'System 1' decision model from ConvAIInnovations that provides mathematically calibrated probabilities for text classification, not generative text.

What to make with it

  • Automate customer support by routing emails to departments or detecting churn risk.
  • Perform content moderation by classifying text for hate speech or spam with calibrated probabilities.
  • Apply guardrails to AI outputs by assessing text for specific criteria.

Skip it if

If your domain requires extremely high accuracy on niche decisions that differ greatly from general knowledge, as it may require fine-tuning.
