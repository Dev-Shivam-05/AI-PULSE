What it is

Anatomy is a free AI skill by wheresryan22 that builds interactive, isometric diagrams of complex systems by inventing physical, working objects to explain concepts.

What to make with it

  • Visualize and interact with a token bucket rate limiter simulation.
  • Explore dynamic WebGL effects like the plume in a 'raptor-engine' example.
  • Understand intricate mechanical systems with animated parts, like a 'turning-dial' with 90 moving parts.

Skip it if

Not for static images. Extreme multi-level zooming into tiny parts may not be perfectly smooth or fully implemented yet.
